## Employee Attrition Analytics & Risk Assessment

### Business Problem
Identify key drivers of employee attrition and proactively flag high-risk employees to support retention planning.

### Approach
- Performed data validation and exploratory analysis across demographics, compensation, tenure, satisfaction, and workload.
- Engineered features and built attrition risk scores to segment employees into low, medium, and critical risk groups.
- Conducted root cause analysis to explain attrition patterns across departments and roles.
- Built interactive Power BI dashboards to support HR decision-making.

### Key Insights
- Attrition peaks in the first 0–2 years of tenure.
- Overtime and low job satisfaction are the strongest attrition drivers.
- Low-income bands show significantly higher attrition risk.

### Business Recommendations
- Reduce overtime through workforce balancing.
- Improve onboarding for early-tenure employees.
- Correct compensation inequities in high-risk segments.
- Conduct targeted retention interventions for critical roles.
